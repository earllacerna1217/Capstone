This capstone project focuses on analyzing sales performance for EmporiUm, a student bookstore chain, with Maine as my assigned sales territory.
Using MySQL, I analyzed sales data from 2022 to 2025, including both in-store and online sales, to identify revenue trends, transaction activity,
product category performance, and store rankings.
I used Microsoft Excel to create visualizations highlighting Maine's contribution to the Northeast region and the sales performance of its physical stores. My analysis identified a 62.7% revenue decline at the Bar Harbor location during Q4 2025, leading to recommendations for further investigation.
The project also includes a PowerPoint presentation summarizing my key findings and business recommendations, 
along with a self-recorded presentation demonstrating my SQL analysis, data visualization process, and interpretation of the results.
